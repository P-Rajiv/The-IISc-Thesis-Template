\noindent
\begin{minipage}[c]{0.55\textwidth}
    Chest X-ray (CXR) images are fundamental diagnostic tools used to identify various
    lung diseases. They provide a non-invasive means for clinicians to visualize
    the internal structures of the chest, facilitating the detection of abnormalities
    such as infections, tumors, or chronic respiratory conditions. This imaging
    modality remains a cornerstone of clinical practice due to its accessibility
    and effectiveness. 
\end{minipage}
